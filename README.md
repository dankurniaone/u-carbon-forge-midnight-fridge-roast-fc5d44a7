# Midnight Fridge Roast

One line roasting a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight:

> I asked the fridge for water at 12:01. It said 'We're closed' and suggested I hydrate during business hours.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, checking the gate first: UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace.
